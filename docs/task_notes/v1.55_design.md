# v1.55 SAM3 Object-Persistence Uplift

## Goal

- Add the smallest local `YOLO26n + SAM3` path needed to test whether segmentation/object persistence is the next real bottleneck.
- Compare `YOLO26n only` against `YOLO26n + SAM3` on fake and real small pilots without widening retrieval/evaluator scope.
- Export a result-layer recommendation that says whether SAM3 should move into the next stage, whether object-memory logic still blocks the gain, or whether the signal gain is too weak to matter.

## residual_risks

- `YOLO26n + SAM3` improved persistence and chain grounding on the real pilot, but it did not improve `object_memory_items_total`; the next bottleneck is likely object-memory aggregation rather than raw segmentation alone.
- `lost_object_query_support_rate` regressed from `1.0` to `0.5` on the real pilot even though persistence improved, so SAM3 should not yet be promoted into the larger main-real path.
- The current result still does not justify widening scope into retrieval or decision changes; the next narrow step should stay on the perception/object-memory boundary.
